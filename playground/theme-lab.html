Contrast check

WCAG AA asks for 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and UI shapes like borders of inputs and focus rings. Fails here mean some people won't be able to read it.

Generated color scales

Each color gets 12 steps, and each step has one job. Components never use a step number directly; they use a semantic name (like text-muted) that points to a step, and dark mode just points the same names to the dark scale.

What you're deciding

Tokens

Named design decisions (accent, space-4, radius-md). Change a token and every component follows, which is why agents should never hard-code a color.

OKLCH color

Lightness, chroma (intensity), hue. Unlike hex or HSL, equal lightness numbers look equally light to the eye, so scales come out even.

The 12 steps

1 to 2 page backgrounds, 3 to 5 control backgrounds (normal, hover, pressed), 6 to 8 borders, 9 to 10 solid fills, 11 to 12 text.

4px grid

All spacing comes from one scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32...). Consistent gaps are most of what makes a UI look "designed".
